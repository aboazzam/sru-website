Admission Process

How Do You Apply?

The SRU admission process is designed to be straightforward and transparent across seven clear steps.

1
Takes: 10 minutes

Create Your Admissions Portal Account

Start by creating your personal account on the SRU Admissions Portal. You'll need an email address and phone number for identity verification.

Use an official email Save a strong password
2
Takes: 20 minutes

Select Your Program & College

Browse the available academic programs and choose the one that aligns with your interests and ambitions. You may select more than one as a secondary preference.

Review program requirements Select up to 3 choices Check acceptance rate
3
Takes: 15 minutes

Complete the Application Form

Enter your personal details, academic information, and standardised test results including the General Aptitude Test and the Achievement Test.

Verify all information Add academic achievements
4
Takes: 30 minutes

Upload Required Documents

Upload clear scanned copies of all required documents. Ensure each document is legible and complete before uploading. Maximum file size per document: 5 MB.

PDF or JPG formats Min. 300 DPI quality
6
Takes: up to 14 business days

Application Review & Decision

The admissions committee reviews all complete applications and issues a decision within 14 business days. You'll receive instant notification via email and SMS.

Non-refundable fee E-receipt provided Track status in portal Email & SMS alerts
7
Final Stage

Confirm Acceptance & Enrol

After receiving your offer letter, confirm your acceptance within the specified deadline, pay the acceptance deposit, then follow the official registration instructions to begin your academic journey.

Confirm within 7 days Attend new student orientation

Admission Documents

Required Documents

Ensure all the following documents are ready before you begin filling the application.

Personal Documents
  • Valid National ID Card (for Saudi citizens) Required
  • Valid Passport (for residents & non-Saudis) Required
  • Recent passport-sized photo (white background) Required
  • Family Status Certificate Optional
Academic Documents
  • High School Certificate (authenticated) Required
  • Detailed Academic Transcript Required
  • General Aptitude Test Result (Qiyas) Required
  • Achievement Test Result (Qiyas) Required
  • Awards & Achievement Certificates (if any) Optional
Language Certificates
  • English Language Certificate (IELTS / TOEFL) Required
  • Arabic Language Certificate (non-native speakers) Required
  • Additional Language Certificates (French, German...) Optional
Health Documents
  • Comprehensive Medical Examination Certificate Required
  • Mandatory Vaccination Record Required
  • Medical Report for Special Conditions (if applicable) Optional
