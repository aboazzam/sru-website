Alumni Benefits

Your SRU Badge — Lifelong Privileges

Graduating from SRU isn't the end of your relationship with the university — it's the start of a series of exclusive privileges designed to support you for life.

Lifetime Digital Library Access
Retain full access to over 500,000 resources in the university's digital library for life as an alumnus.
20% Postgraduate Tuition Discount
Apply to any Masters or PhD programme at SRU and enjoy a 20% tuition discount exclusively for alumni.
Exclusive Professional Network
Connect with 15,000+ alumni through SRU's exclusive professional networking platform and benefit from partnership and collaboration opportunities.
Free Lifelong Career Coaching
Book free career coaching sessions with our specialists at any point in your journey, even years after graduation.
Priority Access to University Events
Receive early invitations and booking priority for all university events, conferences, and graduation ceremonies.
Campus Facilities Access
Enjoy access to university facilities — library, labs, and sports courts — after graduation at discounted rates for network members.
Exclusive Alumni Newsletter
Receive a monthly dedicated newsletter with the latest university news, job opportunities, and highlights from top alumni worldwide.
Annual Alumni Excellence Awards
An annual award honouring distinguished SRU graduates in professional excellence, entrepreneurship, and community responsibility.
