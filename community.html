University's Role

A Foundation Stone in Community Building

Sulaiman Al-Rajhi University plays a pivotal role in advancing sustainable local development across Al-Qassim and the Kingdom, through targeted programs that strengthen partnerships between the university, government bodies, the private sector, and civil society.

We believe the university is not merely an educational institution, but an engine for social and economic transformation — preparing graduates capable of leading development and serving their nation.
